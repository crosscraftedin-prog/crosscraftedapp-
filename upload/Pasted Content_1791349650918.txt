12. CONTENT QUALITY PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Koino Apologetics should be:

- thoughtful
- respectful
- Bible-grounded
- evidence-aware
- intellectually honest
- welcoming to sincere questions

Do not design the CMS around attacking atheists, Muslims, Hindus, Jews or other groups.

When comparing Christianity with another worldview:

focus on beliefs and arguments, not insulting people.

Avoid unsupported claims such as:

"Science has proven Christianity."

Instead allow authors to distinguish:

- evidence
- historical claims
- philosophical arguments
- theological claims
- interpretation
- areas of uncertainty

This is especially important for Science & Faith content.

The platform should allow contributors with different Christian traditions to write while Admin retains editorial control.
